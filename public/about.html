About C4 Ground Control

Excellence in landscaping since 2009

Our Story

C4 Ground Control was founded in 2009 with a simple mission: to transform outdoor spaces throughout Orlando and Central Florida with unmatched quality and professionalism.

Starting with just a small crew and a commitment to excellence, we've grown into one of the region's most trusted landscaping companies. Our reputation is built on delivering exceptional results, treating every property as if it were our own, and standing behind our work with pride.

Today, we're proud to serve residential and commercial clients throughout the Orlando area with a full range of landscaping services including irrigation, sod installation, drainage solutions, landscape lighting, and complete design-build projects.

Why Choose Us

We're not just another landscaping company. Here's what sets us apart:

  • Over 15 years of experience in Central Florida landscaping
  • Fully licensed and insured for your protection
  • Experienced, professional crews who take pride in their work
  • Attention to detail on every project, large or small
  • Quality materials and proven installation methods
  • Transparent pricing with no hidden fees
  • 100% customer satisfaction guarantee
  • On-time project completion

Our commitment to quality has earned us hundreds of satisfied customers and a portfolio of beautiful landscapes across Orlando and the surrounding areas.
